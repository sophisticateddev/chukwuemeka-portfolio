## Rules

- Headings are balanced (`text-wrap: balance`) so lines come out even; paragraphs avoid a lone last word (`text-wrap: pretty`).
- Numbers that animate use tabular figures so they don't jiggle.
- Secondary text uses the `muted` token, which passes AA on every surface.
